Building Strength 101

Published: April 4, 2025 | Author: Coach John Steel

Strength training is more than lifting heavy things—it's about understanding your body, movement mechanics, and consistency. In this guide, we'll walk you through the essentials of getting started on your strength journey.

Why Strength Training?

Strength training improves muscle tone, bone density, and even mental health. Whether you're training for a sport or everyday life, strength makes everything better.

The Big Three Lifts

Start with the basics: Squat, Bench Press, and Deadlift. These compound movements engage multiple muscle groups and form the foundation of effective strength routines.

Tips for Beginners

  • Focus on form over weight.
  • Stick to a consistent schedule (3x per week is great).
  • Track your progress over time.
  • Fuel your body with proper nutrition and rest.
